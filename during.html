During Your Experience

Navigate your active project through adaptive project management, human-centered fieldwork, and constructive feedback. Use this page to choose tools for research, progress tracking, and communication with your partner.

Adapt as your project develops

Real-world projects rarely follow a straight line. Requirements may change, team dynamics may evolve, and partners may face unexpected constraints. Research can also reveal needs that challenge your original assumptions.

This phase is an opportunity to practice adaptability and ethical decision-making. Revisit project boundaries when requests grow, use feedback to improve your work, and connect technical choices to the needs of the people affected.

Focus on three areas of active work

  • Fieldwork and research: Use human-centered methods to understand the context and develop insights.
  • Sponsor and client updates: Maintain transparency through regular progress reporting.
  • Feedback: Gather, evaluate, and incorporate input during the project.

Organize observations and research insights

HFLI AEIOU Worksheet for Observations

Use this framework during field observation to organize notes into Activities, Environments, Interactions, Objects, and Users. These categories help you pay attention to different aspects of the setting.

HFLI User-Needs-Insights Worksheet

Use this worksheet after interviews and observations to turn raw findings into user needs and actionable design insights. Bring those insights into your team's next discussion of the work.

Use feedback and examine ethical challenges

HFLI Feedback Worksheet

Use this worksheet to structure midpoint feedback among students, instructors, and partners. Identify what needs attention before the next phase of work.

Ginsberg Center Community Engagement Cases Handout

Use the case scenarios to discuss ethical dilemmas and possible ways to respond. Consider the perspectives of the people affected before deciding how a lesson applies to your project.

Keep your partner informed and your team organized

Sponsor Dashboard Template from Organizational Excellence

Use this high-level dashboard to communicate project status to external clients and help them understand the overall health of the project.

Student Project Communications Plan Example and Template

Use this resource to agree on meeting frequency, communication channels, and expected response times. Revisit the plan when the team's needs change.

Project Management Worksheet

Use this worksheet to break project goals into milestones and task assignments. Review it alongside your progress updates so the next steps remain clear.

Access the active-project resources

Ask your instructor or the Engaged Learning Office for the worksheets and templates named above. Include the resource title in your request so they can help you locate the appropriate material.

Email umsi.engagement@umich.edu for help locating ELO resources.

Choose a next step when the work changes

Use these prompts with the resources above to guide a team check-in.

  • New findings: What did our observations or interviews reveal, and how does that affect our understanding of user needs?
  • Changing scope: Does a new request fit the agreement we made with the partner? What needs to be discussed before we commit?
  • Feedback: What have students, instructors, or partners asked us to reconsider?
  • Communication: Who needs an update, through which channel, and when?
  • Next actions: Which milestones and task assignments need to be updated?

Revisit your partnership agreements when expectations need clarification, or return to your preparation and learning goals.
